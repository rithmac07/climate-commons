About this data

A temperature anomaly shows the difference between a year's global surface temperature and the 1951–1980 average. Positive values are warmer than that baseline; negative values are cooler.

Climate Commons uses NASA GISTEMP v4 data. NASA describes GISTEMP v4 as an estimate of global surface temperature change and provides the underlying data and methodology publicly.

Data source: NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies, GISTEMP v4. This site presents temperature anomalies, not local weather forecasts.
